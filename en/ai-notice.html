1. Aria operates on generative AI

Aria’s AI features run on generative AI models developed by other companies.

2. How AI output is shown

  • AI replies appear in chat as messages from the assistant “Aria”, and briefings appear as briefing cards, visually distinct from text you write.
  • When AI organizes tasks or notes, or creates or changes events, the result is shown, for example as a result card, so you can review it.
  • If you copy or share AI output outside the app, we recommend saying that it was written by AI so that recipients are not misled.

3. AI is at the core of the Service and works only after you agree

  • Aria is a personal assistant built on generative AI that understands your tasks, calendar and notes to give you tailored briefings, memory and connected notes, so AI processing is an essential part of the Service. For that reason the app has no separate setting that stops AI features on their own.
  • Before anything is sent to an AI provider, the app explains what information is sent to which provider and when (including the destination country, the retention period and what happens if you decline) and asks for your explicit consent. New users consent in the AI step of initial setup; existing users who have not yet consented (including those who had AI features off in an earlier version) see a consent screen the next time they open the app. Nothing is sent to an AI provider before you consent.
  • People under 14 may not use the Service. On the consent screen, you must confirm “I'm 14 or older” before you can tap “Agree and Start”. Users who consented in an earlier version without this age check are asked to confirm their age once on the consent screen the next time they open the app, and nothing is sent to AI providers until they do.
  • You may decline, but if you do, you cannot use Aria. Without consenting, you can still go from the consent screen to Settings to manage or delete your account.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time. Deleting your account and data in Settings → Account → “Delete account” or “Delete all my data” also ends transfers to AI providers. To stop AI processing while keeping your account, email our privacy officer; you then cannot use Aria until you consent again (Privacy Policy, Sections 1.2 and 10).

4. Limits of AI output

  • AI output is generated probabilistically and may be wrong, out of date, incomplete or biased.
  • Always confirm medical, legal, tax, financial and safety matters with a qualified professional. AI output is not professional advice.
  • In an emergency, do not rely on AI; contact your local emergency services immediately.
  • Aria is a personal productivity service that does not make decisions with a significant impact on people’s lives, safety or rights, such as hiring, credit or medical diagnosis. We operate it as a service that is not high-impact AI under the Act.

5. How your data is handled

  • When you use AI features, the information needed for the request (the message you send, photos or a current location you attach, related tasks, notes, events, memories, etc.) is sent to the AI provider through our server. Your account identifier and email are not sent.
  • Neither we nor the AI providers use your content to train AI models.
  • Everything is sent over encrypted connections (TLS), and our server uses per-account access control (row-level security) so that other users cannot see your data. When you delete your account, your data on our server is deleted too (Privacy Policy, Sections 2 and 7).
  • What is sent, international transfers and retention periods are described in Sections 1 and 5 of our Privacy Policy.

6. Reporting inappropriate AI replies

If the AI gives an inappropriate or harmful reply, touch and hold the reply in the chat (on Mac, right-click) and choose “Report Inappropriate Response”. An email opens, pre-filled with the reply time, app version and reply text, which you can review or edit before sending. You can also write to contact@lipson.kr directly (with a screenshot if possible). We will review it and take appropriate action.
